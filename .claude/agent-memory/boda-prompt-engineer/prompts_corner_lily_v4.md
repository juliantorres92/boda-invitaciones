# Esquinas Florales v4 — Prompts Completos

**Reglas invariables**: Lilium candidum = flor principal siempre. Composicion en L estricta. Fondo transparente PNG. Watercolor botanica. Ramas finas como lineas de lapiz.

**Keywords de tamano**: `large prominent blooms`, `close-up botanical view`, `oversized lily blooms`, `flowers filling the frame`, `bold floral arrangement`

**Keywords de follaje**: `sparse airy trailing sprigs`, `long gracefully elongated trailing stems`, `wispy delicate greenery`, `breathing space between leaves`, `loose trailing stems with visible gaps`, `not dense not bushy`, `stems extending and flowing naturally`, `elongated flowing branches with natural curves`

**Keywords de ramas alargadas**: `long trailing stems that extend well beyond the flower clusters`, `gracefully elongated branches flowing outward`, `not short or compact stems`, `naturally curving elongated vines`

**Instruccion de follaje obligatoria** (incluir en cada prompt):
> Foliage should be light and airy with breathing space between elements — individual leaves and long, gracefully elongated trailing stems visible against transparent background, not dense or bushy. Stems and branches should be elongated and flowing, trailing gracefully with natural curves, extending well beyond the flower clusters — not short or compact.

**Negative prompt estandar (Midjourney)**:
```

geometric modern cartoon centered symmetrical sunflowers tulips diagonal sweep
centered bouquet oval arrangement small flowers tiny blooms distant flowers
dense foliage heavy greenery thick branches bushy leaves clustered leaves

```

## Tips de iteracion

- **Flores aun pequenas en DALL-E**: Anadir al inicio `"Extreme close-up watercolor botanical view."`
- **L no se mantiene en MJ**: Anadir antes de `--no`: `strict L-shaped composition only, one arm horizontal along bottom edge, one arm vertical along left edge, NO diagonal sweep, NO centered bouquet`
- **Color secundario muy saturado**: Anadir `very muted, heavily desaturated, faded washed out watercolor tones, aged antique feel`
- **Lirios no reconocibles**: Anadir `Lilium candidum, trumpet-shaped white lily flowers with visible stamens, clearly recognizable lily blooms`
- **Follaje demasiado denso**: Anadir `sparse wispy greenery, individual trailing stems with gaps, not bushy or clustered, breathing space between leaves`
- **Ramas/tallos demasiado cortos o compactos**: Anadir `long gracefully elongated trailing stems extending well beyond flower clusters, not short or compact, naturally curving elongated vines flowing outward with organic movement`
- **Colores siguen vividos despues de ajustar prompt**: Usar post-procesamiento: reducir saturacion -20% en editor de imagen, aplicar filtro sepia suave
